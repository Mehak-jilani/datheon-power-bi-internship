Top 5 Retention Recommendations
  1. First-90-days onboarding — 0–12 mo cohort 47.4% churn. Welcome call + setup help + first-bill explainer.
  2. Month-to-month → Annual — 42.7% vs 2.8%. Offer discount at month 3–6 to lock 1-year.
  3. Bundle Tech Support with Fiber — Fiber + No Support = 49.4% churn. Free support 3 months.
  4. Auto-pay migration — Electronic check 45.3% vs auto-pay ~15%. Auto-pay discount.
  5. Save-play for high bill — MonthlyCharges > $85 + tenure < 12 mo → loyalty discount / plan review.
