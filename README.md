## Task 4 — Financial Margin Dashboard

HTML dashboard. File is in the repo — `Task_4_Financial_Margin_Dashboard/Task4_Dashboard.html`

**Data:** `financial_sample.xlsx` — 700 rows, 2013–2014

### Verified results (cross-checked from Excel)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Gross Sales | $127.93M |
| Discounts | $9.21M (7.2% of gross) |
| **Net Sales** | **$118.73M** — exact: $118,726,350.26 |
| COGS | $101.83M (85.8% of net sales) |
| **Profit** | **$16.89M** — exact: $16,893,702.26 |
| Profit Margin | **14.23%** |
| Units Sold | 1.13M |

### What it covers

- **Filters:** Year, Segment, Country — KPIs, charts, product table and insights update together
- **Net Sales & Margin by Segment**
    - Government: $52,504,260.67 sales, 21.69% margin — 44% of total sales
    - Small Business: $42,427,918.50 sales, 9.77% margin
    - Enterprise: $19,611,694.38 sales, **-$614,545.62 profit, -3.13% margin**
    - Midmarket: $2,381,883.08 sales, 27.71% margin
    - Channel Partners: $1,800,593.64 sales, **73.13% margin**
- **Profit by Discount Band**
    - None: $1,736,455.00 profit, 21.86% margin
    - Low: $6,188,857.70 profit, 17.87% margin
    - Medium: $5,579,522.83 profit, 14.39% margin
    - High: $3,388,866.73 profit, **9.07% margin**
- **Monthly Net Sales — 2013 vs 2014**, **Net Sales by Country**, **Product Performance**
    - Lowest product: Carretera — 13.22% margin

### Executive takeaways

- Enterprise is loss-making: -$0.61M profit — heavy discounting erases its revenue.
- Channel Partners is the most profitable segment: 73% margin.
- Government drives the business: $52.50M net sales at a healthy 22% margin.
- High discount band has the thinnest margin (9%).
